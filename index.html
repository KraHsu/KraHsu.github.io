01 NOW

A SMALL INTRODUCTION

Curious about the gap between thinking and touching.

I am a PhD student researching Physical AI — systems that perceive, reason, and act in the real world. My work lives somewhere between robotics, learning, simulation, and the messy details of making an idea actually run.

When I am away from the lab, I design interfaces, maintain open-source tools, and obsess over the little moments that make software feel calm, clear, and surprisingly nice to use.
